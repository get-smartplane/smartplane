# Smartplane design

Clear skies, as a work tool. Paper carries the reading, navy ink carries text and the main button, and the
sky (pixel clouds plus the outlined paper plane) is kept for the edges: the landing hero, sign-in,
onboarding, empty states and the overview's header band. The source of truth is the CSS:
`apps/web/src/index.css` (app) and, in get-smartplane/landing, `src/styles/global.css` + `motion.css` (site). This file
explains it; if they disagree, fix one of them.

## Type

Three faces, self-hosted from `/fonts/` (all SIL OFL 1.1). Never load them from a CDN.

| Face | Use | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Bricolage Grotesque** (variable) | Titles, big numbers, the wordmark | Site h1–h3: weight 680, tracking −0.035em. App `font-display`: 650, −0.03em. Statements: 460, −0.012em |
| **Hanken Grotesk** (variable) | Everything people read: body, UI, buttons | Default `font-sans` |
| **JetBrains Mono** | Real code only: config, CLI, tool calls, ids, hashes | Never for decoration or labels |

Site type scale, five sizes only: `hero` (clamp 2.75–6.5rem, line-height 0.95), `head` (2.25–4.5rem, 0.98),
`statement` (1.25–1.625rem, 1.3), `body` (1.0625–1.125rem, 1.55), `small` (0.9375rem, 1.45).
Headings use `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `pretty`. Numbers in tables and charts are `tabular`.

## Colour

| Token | Light | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Ink / primary | `#0f2140` navy | Text, main buttons, the wordmark |
| Ink soft | `#44587a` (site), `#4d6080` (app muted) | Secondary text |
| Paper | `#fafcfe`, sheets `#ffffff`, paper-2 `#edf2f8` | Surfaces |
| Sky | `#a9d2f3`, deep `#74b5ea`, pale `#e4f0fb` | The sky and the info tone; the icon tile is `#74b5ea` |
| Line | `#0b0b0c` | Plane and checkpoint outlines |
| Cobalt | `#2457c5` | **Cleared**: allowed, active, intact; links and focus |
| Amber | `#e39a1c` (ink `#6e4300`) | **Held**: pending, draft, needs approval |
| Red | `#c4342b` (ink `#9a2018`) | **Blocked**: denied, broken, removed |

Each status colour means exactly one thing; never use cobalt, amber or red decoratively. Sky-blue info
means "observing only" (monitor mode). Dark mode is a night sky: navy surfaces stepped by tone
(`#0a1630` → `#0f1e3a` → `#132544`), never grey. Every colour is a token; no raw hex in components.

## Shape and space

- Corners are tight: `--radius` 0.3rem (about 4px), close to the site's square buttons. No pill cards.
- Site rhythm: `--margin` clamp(16–36px), `--gutter` clamp(20–48px), `--section` clamp(96–180px);
  content max-width 1480px (`.wrap`).
- Borders are hairlines (`--border` `#dfe6ef`). No drop-shadow stacks or glassmorphism.
- Layouts work at phone width with a 16px side gutter and no horizontal scroll.

## The plane and the logo

The mark is a two-panel paper plane, outlined near-black (`#0b0b0c`), panels `#ffffff` and `#edf2f8`:

```svg
<g stroke="#0b0b0c" stroke-width="1.6" stroke-linejoin="round">
  <path d="M3 17 29 7 14 19Z" fill="#fff"/><path d="M14 19 29 7 17 27Z" fill="#edf2f8"/>
</g>
```

Files in `public/img/brand/` of get-smartplane/landing:

- `icon.svg`, `icon-512.png`: the plane on a sky tile (`#74b5ea`, rx 7 on a 32 grid). Favicon, avatars.
- `logo-stacked.svg`, `logo-stacked-sky.svg`: "Smart" over "Plane" in Bricolage 680, navy, with the plane
  flying across the gap. Text is outlined, so no font is needed. Transparent and on-sky versions.

The name is written **Smartplane** in copy (one word, one capital). The wordmark may stack it as Smart / Plane.
Keep the plane's outline; don't recolour it, add gradients or rotate it off its heading.

## Sky scenes

Pixel clouds (lit `#ffffff`, half `#eef3fa`, shade `#d9e4f1`) on the sky ramp. They are illustrations for
edges and moments, not wallpaper behind working screens. The AuthKit background is
`public/img/sky/authkit-bg.{webp,png}` in get-smartplane/landing, generated by its `scripts/authkit-bg.ts`.

## Motion

- **App:** fast (under 200ms), decelerating (`--ease-out-quint` `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), and
  only where it explains something: a surface arriving, a section unfolding, a press registering.
  Keyboard-opened surfaces and frequent toggles (tabs, filters) change instantly.
- **Site:** reveals on scroll (opacity and translate only, 700–800ms, staggered 80–90ms), slow cloud drift.
  Hidden states apply only under `html.js`, so content shows without scripts.
- Transform and opacity only. No bounce or overshoot on UI. `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps the content
  and drops the travel.

## Product imagery

Screenshots and homepage panels show the product's real components without the sidebar, cropped to
the part that tells the story. Third-party logos are self-hosted in get-smartplane/landing under `public/img/logos`.

## Voice

- Plain words, short sentences, the reader's job first. "Say the thing, not the tool."
- Name things as the product does: resources, grants, rules (or "guard rules"), **Verify** (not
  "compile"), monitor and block modes, machine tokens.
- A dry, confident wit is welcome in marketing ("Keep your secrets – secret."); the app stays matter-of-fact.
- Errors say what happened and what to do next; never blame the user, never show stack traces.
- Never claim a certification or guarantee the product doesn't have (see the claims ledger, `docs/legal/README.md` in get-smartplane/landing).
